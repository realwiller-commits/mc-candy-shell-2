# MC Candy Shell - Rap Battle Mod

A Minecraft 26.3 Fabric mod where you rap battle an original boss, **MC Candy Shell**, a glossy green candy rapper.
Pick 1 of 4 rhymes each round. Good rhymes drain his health bar, but the battle is rigged: you always lose in the end.

## How to play (current prototype)

- `/candyshell start` begins a battle
- `/candyshell pick 1` (or 2, 3, 4) picks your rhyme
